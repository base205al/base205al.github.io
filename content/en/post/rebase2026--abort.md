# re:BASE --abort

After careful consideration, the BASE team has decided to move re:BASE to 2027.

We want re:BASE to be an event that brings together Birmingham's software community for a meaningful day of learning, collaboration, and connection. To give the event the attention and resources it deserves, we're taking additional time to plan for its return next year.

While we're disappointed that re:BASE won't take place this year, we're excited about the opportunity to build an even stronger event for 2027.

## What's Next?
BASE remains committed to supporting and growing Birmingham's software community. We'll continue hosting [events, meetups, and other initiatives (RSS)](/tags/events/feed.xml) that align with our mission and values. We encourage our community members to [stay engaged](/contact/), provide feedback, and participate in future activities organized by BASE.

To stay up to date on upcoming events and initiatives, visit [base205al.org](/) or get in touch with the BASE team.

Thank you to everyone who has supported re:BASE and BASE. We look forward to bringing the community together for re:BASE in 2027.

Thank you for your support and understanding.
