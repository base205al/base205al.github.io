# Software in the 'Ham

BASE is Birmingham Area Software Enthusiasts, a community serving seasoned and unseasoned practitioners of software engineering in Birmingham, Alabama, and surrounds. We’re focused on education and connection, helping software people at any level grow technologically and meets others who share their interests.

## Our Mission

BASE is dedicated to fostering a vibrant and inclusive software development community in the Birmingham area. We believe technology, and technologists, thrive when diverse minds, experiences, and views come together, so that’s what we do: provide a supportive environment where software enthusiasts of literally — literally — any skill level, from the newest noob to the most seasoned pro, can expand their knowledge, grow their career, and dig deep into the Birmingham tech ecosystem.

## What We Do

### Educate
Regular meetups, workshops, and presentations on topics ranging from programming languages and frameworks to DevOps and cloud technologies to emerging technologies and industry trends. Learn, teach, do a little of both.

### Connect
Networking with like-minded professionals, students, and hobbyists. Working together on open-source initiatives and community-driven software projects. Hanging out and talking about work (or things entirely unrelated to work).

### Grow
Mentorship programs pairing experienced developers with those just starting out. Career development support — job placement assistance, resume review, interview prep.

## Welcome, World!
We welcome developers, designers, project managers, students, career changers, and anyone curious about technology. Whether you're a seasoned professional or writing your first "Hello, World!" program, you have a place in our community.

Join us for our regular meetups, on our [Discord server](./contact/), [contribute to our projects](https://github.com/orgs/base205al), or volunteer to share your expertise with others. 

BASE is what we make it together: a community where Birmingham's software talent can flourish and grow.
