> Please stay tuned for future updates and events from BASE.

# re:BASE --abort

After some deliberation, the BASE team has decided that re:BASE cannot be delivered with the quality and experience that our attendees, speakers, and sponsors, deserve. We deeply regret having to make this decision, but we believe it is the best course of action to maintain the standards and values of our community.

## What Happened?

The decision to cancel re:BASE 2026 was not made lightly. We encountered several challenges that made it clear that we would not be able to deliver the event at the level of quality and experience that our community deserves. 

These challenges, in the order of their impact, were:
- Time constraints
  - The re:BASE team has run multiple events larger than this in the past, and clearly understands the necessary time constraints for this effort, but were subsequently unable to meet those requirements.  
  - This isn't a reflection of the dedication or effort of the team, but rather an acknowledgment of the complexity and scale of organizing such an event.
  - BASE is a volunteer-only organization, and things outside of this effort took precedence.
- Financial constraints
  - While the funding costs for sponsorship were not extraordinary, the overall funding did not meet the necessary levels to support the event as planned.
  - This shortfall in funding made it difficult to plan and execute the event at the desired level of quality and experience.
- Content constraints
  - The CFP for re:BASE 2026 received an extremely limited number of submissions
  - This limited response indicated a lack of sufficient content to build a robust and engaging agenda
  - With such a lack of content, it would have been challenging to curate a high-quality program for the event.
  - Note that the lack of content probably had more to do with our ability to properly market and reach potential speakers than with the interest in participating in re:BASE 2026 itself.

## What Did We Learn?

The cancellation of re:BASE 2026 has provided us with several important lessons:
- Proper time management and realistic planning are crucial for the successful execution of any event.
- Securing adequate funding and sponsorship is essential to ensure the quality and experience of the event.  This wasn't news, by a long stretch, but it served as a stark reminder of its importance.
- Effective marketing and outreach are necessary to attract sufficient content and participation from the community.  We have since engaged new parties to help with this.
- Transparency and communication with the community are vital when making difficult decisions.  Our efforts to keep our community informed throughout this process were not sufficient.
- Volunteer capacity and resource allocation must be carefully considered to avoid overextending the team.

We apologize for any effect this may have caused and appreciate the understanding and support of our community. We look forward to bringing you high-quality events and experiences in the future.

Thank you for being a part of the BASE community. Your engagement and enthusiasm are what drive us to continue creating valuable experiences for everyone involved.

## Where Do We Go From Here?

While re:BASE 2026 will not be happening, the BASE team remains committed to supporting and growing the local software community. We will continue to explore opportunities for [events, meetups, and other initiatives (RSS)](/tags/events/feed.xml) that align with our mission and values. We encourage our community members to [stay engaged](/contact/), provide feedback, and participate in future activities organized by BASE.

As stated above, we have learned valuable lessons from this experience and are taking steps to improve our planning, marketing, and communication efforts for future events.  We are committed to improving our marketing efforts, and we have added new staffing for that.  We are committed to improving our funding efforts, such that we could perhaps self-fund such an event in the future.  We are exploring avenues of such funding to see if they are available. 

Most of all , we are looking forward to delivering a high-quality re:BASE event in the future, alongside BASE's other intiatives and activities.

Thank you for your support and understanding.
