# Join in

Meet people, learn things, and be a part of a thriving tech community.

### Speak up: Show and Tell
The *tech* in *tech community*. The BASE community has a lot to offer, and we want everyone to be able to experience it.
{{< rawhtml >}}
<img src="/images/s_and_t_crazy.png" width="360" align="left" style="float: left; margin-right: 25px;" alt="Description" />
<p>BASE's monthly Show and Tell session is a chance for our enthusiasts to bring what they're working on and spend 5 minutes showing it to the rest of us.</p>

<p>Share what you've been building. See what others have been building. Get input on things you're working on and feedback on things that aren't working. Make connections. Learn things. To crib from Chris Winslett's post in Magic City Tech's Slack channel: Live demos are encouraged, broken demos are cheered.
<br clear="left" />
{{< /rawhtml >}}

Show up to watch or, even better, Show and Tell yourself.

#### The Rules For Showing

- Bring something you're interested in showing to other people. Show it to them. You'll get at least 5 minutes, depending on how may people are presenting, and possibly longer.
- Do a demo, if it can be demo'd. If your demo fails, you will get applause.  
- There might be questions. Stunned silence is also a possibility.
- If you want feedback, ask for it.

#### The Rules for Being Shown To

- Be respectful. Someone is taking time to show you something. It may not be relevant to you, and you may not be able to provide feedback.  
- Be constructive. If you're going to comment on all the work you didn't do, make that comment useful.  Add to the work.

#### Show up

Show and Tell is *generally* scheduled for the first Tuesday of every month, *most often* at Innovation Depot. Watch our front page or find us on [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/base205/events/) to confirm date and location.
